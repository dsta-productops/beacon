## Three crafts, one product

Product, design, and engineering are the three crafts that turn a problem into a working product. Product owns vision, strategy, and the roadmap. Design owns the experience and the research that validates the problem. Engineering owns the architecture and the technical standards. No single person holds all three at the depth each demands, so the product team is built around all three from the start.

## Where the roles map to the Quality Model

This structure is why the [Quality Model](/quality-model) divides functional code (owned by Design) from NFR code (owned by Engineering), and why the assurance layers span both. Product sets the outcome those two streams are ultimately measured against.
