## Getting started

### What problem does BEACON solve? {#why-beacon}

User research, design and measurement are often slow, inconsistent from team to team, and hard to trace back to evidence. BEACON gives every team the same pipeline and tools, from first research to a published result:

- [CLARA](/tools/clara) drafts research and product documents from your Knowledge Base, such as syntheses, personas, PRDs and one-pagers, and cites the evidence behind them.
- INSIGHT gives you live prompts and a transcript during interviews.
- [PRIZM](/tools/prizm) is DSTA's design system, so prototypes built with Claude Code follow DSTA standards.
- [DASH](/tools/dash) measures usability before and after, so you can show your design is better.
- [PULSE](https://pulse-reportcards.vercel.app) publishes a report card that sums up what your product has achieved.

AI does the drafting, and people do the judging.

To find out more about Spectrum, DSTA's platform for product practice, visit [spectrum-dsta.vercel.app](https://spectrum-dsta.vercel.app/).

### How is this different from just using an AI chatbot? {#vs-chatbot}

CLARA and PRIZM come with specialised skills: detailed, step-by-step instructions that tell the AI exactly how to do each job. CLARA has a skill for each artefact, such as a PRD or a persona. It works from your own Knowledge Base, cites its sources, flags where evidence is thin, and files every output in a predictable place. PRIZM's skills tell the AI which components, tokens and rules to use, so prototypes follow DSTA standards. A general-purpose chatbot has none of this built in, so you would have to explain it all yourself, every time.

### Do I need a background in product management or design? {#background}

No. The tutorial walks you through every step. You do need to review everything CLARA produces before you rely on it.

### How long does the tutorial take? {#tutorial-length}

_To be confirmed._ You do not have to finish it in one go. Take it a few steps at a time.

## Access and setup

### I do not use Confluence. Can I still use BEACON? {#not-confluence}

Yes. CLARA also works with GitLab wikis and Plane, and BEACON has a [tutorial for GitLab](/tutorial-gitlab) too. You can also store your artefacts locally, in the folder on your computer that you have allowed CLARA to access.

## Security, data and classification

### What classification of information can I use with CLARA? {#classification}

It depends on the environment you use CLARA in. If you use CLARA in the Internet environment, make sure all content is declassified and desensitised accordingly before it goes in.

### Is my report card public? {#report-card-public}

A published report card on PULSE is public by default. Before you publish, check that everything on it is of an appropriate classification, and check with your programme manager. If it should not be open to the public, publish it privately instead.

### Does INSIGHT record the people I interview? {#insight-recording}

INSIGHT transcribes the conversation on your computer, and nothing leaves your device. It is important to inform your interviewees, before you start, that the session is being transcribed with INSIGHT.

## Trusting the AI output

### What if CLARA gets something wrong or makes something up? {#ai-mistakes}

CLARA cites a source for every finding, and marks anything uncertain as thin, open, provisional or contested. It is told never to invent figures, names or costs. You still review every draft before it is filed, and you are accountable for what you publish.

### Can I edit what CLARA produces? {#edit-output}

Yes. Edit the page directly, or tell CLARA what to change. If you re-run CLARA on the same artefact, it keeps your edits and updates the rest.

### Are 5 to 7 interviews really enough? {#how-many-interviews}

For finding patterns, usually yes. Interviews tell you why people behave as they do, and new themes become rare after a handful of conversations. The DASH survey adds the numbers.

## The process

### Do I have to follow every step in order? {#step-order}

Each step feeds the next, so the order matters when you start from scratch. On a real project, start from wherever your existing material lets you.

### Why does the tutorial write the PRD before the one-pager? {#prd-before-one-pager}

In the tutorial, CLARA builds the one-pager from your PRD, so most of it fills in for you. On a real programme you often start the one-pager early, to get approval and funding, and refine it as the PRD and research mature.

### When should I start a new PRD instead of updating the old one? {#new-prd}

Start a new PRD when the problem, the users or the value metric has changed, when you pivot to a fundamentally different solution, or when the next prototype is a separate component or workstream. Otherwise, keep updating the one you have.

### Can I skip the baseline survey? {#skip-baseline}

Not if you want to show that your design is better. The comparison in step 17 needs a baseline to compare against.

### What if I cannot interview real users? {#no-users}

Approach your ops manager for access to real users. If real users are not available, look for proxy users: people who know the users' work closely enough to speak for them. Good proxy users:

- have done the job themselves recently, such as former operators or instructors who trained on the same systems
- work alongside the users every day, such as supervisors, team leads or technicians who support them
- have seen the work first-hand in the same environment, not just read about it

Treat what proxy users tell you with more caution than first-hand accounts. Note in each field note that the interviewee was a proxy, and check their key points with real users when you can.

## The tools

### Can I turn my prototype into the real product? {#prototype-production}

Not directly. The prototype is for testing ideas with users, so it skips what a live system needs, such as security, accreditation, real data and integrations with other systems. Your engineering team builds the production version properly, and can reuse the PRIZM components and the PRD you wrote along the way.
