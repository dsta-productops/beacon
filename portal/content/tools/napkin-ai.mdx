## What it does

Napkin turns text into diagrams. Type a description of a process, a system or a relationship, and Napkin draws it as a clean diagram that you can edit and export.

Most diagrams in early-phase work are wrong by the next review anyway. Napkin makes them cheaper to draw in the first place, so teams are more willing to revise them.

## When to use it

- Design phase for engineering teams: system diagrams, data flows, and breaking a capability into parts
- Any time a team is working out how things relate and a shared picture would help the conversation
- Stakeholder presentations, when a diagram lasts better than bullet points on a slide

## How to access

[napkin.ai](https://napkin.ai). Web-based.

## Output expectations

- Treat outputs as starting points. Most diagrams need one round of human edits before they communicate clearly
