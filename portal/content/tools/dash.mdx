**Current version:** DASH 2.0.

## What it does

DASH is a purpose-built measurement platform for both digital and engineering teams. It offers measures suited to each:

- **Digital teams:** user experience and product measures, such as the System Usability Scale (a short score of how easy a system is to use), Customer Satisfaction, and OKRs (goals with measurable targets).
- **Engineering teams:** capability measures.

DASH is the canonical home (the main, authoritative place) for answering "did the work achieve its intended outcome?" Every document produced in the Measure phase connects back to a DASH measurement framework.

DASH also supports baselines. Early in Research, a programme can run surveys against the existing, older or competing system. Later measurements of the new system then have real figures to compare against.

## When to use it

- Research phase: set a baseline for the existing, older or competing system, so future success criteria have real figures to compare against
- Measure phase, for both teams: run prototype surveys and system surveys on the new product
- Throughout the product's life: DASH is not only for one-off tests, because it tracks outcomes continuously
- Before an acquisition decision (engineering): to base the decision on measured evidence of capability
- After launch (digital): to check whether the outcomes in the PRD are being achieved

## How to access

Available at [diux-dash.com](https://diux-dash.com). Each programme has a DASH workspace.

## Output expectations

- Each measure is tied to a specific success criterion, not chosen because it is easy to measure
- Reports show trends and decisions, not just numbers
