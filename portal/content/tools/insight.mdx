## What it does

An interview assistant that listens while you talk with a user and suggests one thing at a time: a follow-up question, a definition for jargon, or a snippet from your own documents. It keeps a running summary and a glossary so you can keep your place, and writes a transcript with each speaker labelled.

INSIGHT runs on your computer without internet access. Nothing leaves your device.

## When to use it

- Research phase — during user interviews, for live prompts and a transcript
- Before an interview — upload your PRD or brief and add your team's terms, so the suggestions match your project
- After an interview — export the review as Markdown and paste it into a Field Notes page in your Knowledge Base

## How to access

INSIGHT is a Mac app, available as a `.dmg` download. It is not yet available on Windows. The tutorial's step 9 walks through installing it.

Tell your interviewees, before you start, that the session is being transcribed with INSIGHT.

## Output expectations

- A transcript with each speaker labelled, and suggestions tied to timestamps
- Notes filed exactly as they were taken, so the research synthesis is not biased by tidied-up notes
