## What it does

Forma is an AI-assisted tool for modelling spaces and platforms. Engineering teams use it to model environments, make platform fly-throughs, and show capabilities in their physical setting.

Luma generates content from a plain-language description, and Napkin works at the level of diagrams. Forma works with structured models of space. This makes it useful when the team needs to reason about where things are placed, their dimensions, and how they interact in space.

## When to use it

- Design phase for engineering teams
- Developing capabilities with a spatial side, where physical setting, placement or scale is part of the capability
- Combining capabilities across platforms, when you need to model how one capability interacts with others in physical space

## How to access

[autodesk.com/products/forma](https://autodesk.com/products/forma). Cloud-hosted.

- Treat models as conceptual unless they are checked against engineering specifications
