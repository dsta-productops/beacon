## What it does

Dynatrace is a platform that records how people use live digital products, with an AI layer that helps pull out insights. Any team member can ask questions in plain language and get useful answers, without specialist data-analysis skills.

The AI layer is what lets product owners and designers use Dynatrace directly. Asking "where are users dropping off in the onboarding flow?" in plain language is faster than writing a query. It also brings up what matters when the team did not know which question to ask.

## When to use it

- Measure phase for digital teams: measuring live products
- Throughout a digital product's life: tracking outcomes continuously
- Supporting decisions: when a team needs evidence to decide whether to iterate, harden or sunset a product (keep improving it, make it more robust, or retire it)

## How to access

Available through the MCC tooling stack today, and planned for ANZ C and ANZ S. It is set up for each programme, and the AI query layer is available from the standard Dynatrace screen.

## Output expectations

- It gives numbers on live use, such as sessions, paths, drop-offs and error rates, but they are only as good as the usage recording set up in the product
- AI-generated answers are starting points, not final analyses. Check them against the underlying data before acting
- Pair the usage data with [DASH](/tools/dash) survey results. What people do, plus what they say, is stronger than either alone
- Compare against the PRD's success criteria, not against raw counts on their own
