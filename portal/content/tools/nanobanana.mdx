## What it does

Nano Banana is an AI image generator. Engineering teams use it to make visual references for operational scenarios, illustrations of capabilities, and concept images that operators can react to.

Its strength in the Pipeline is speed. A vague capability concept quickly becomes a concrete picture that an operator can point at and say "yes, like that" or "no, the problem is here."

## When to use it

- Design phase for engineering teams
- Early concept development, when the team needs something visual to anchor conversations with operators
- Before acquisition: to make trade-offs visible before they are locked in

## How to access

[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com). Cloud-hosted.

## Output expectations

- Treat outputs as illustrations, not specifications. They are conversation starters, not binding requirements
- Pair with an operational scenario from the [operational-scenario generator](/prompts/operational-scenario-generator), to give narrative context
