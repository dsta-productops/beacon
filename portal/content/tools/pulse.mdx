## What it does

Gives every product the same one-page report card: the problem it solves, what it has achieved, how healthy it is and what changed lately. Because every card follows the same format, products can be compared.

Every figure on a report card carries an evidence label, such as Measured, Baseline, Illustrative or Not measured yet, so readers can tell a real result from an estimate.

## When to use it

- Measure phase — once you have test results to report
- Throughout a product's lifetime — keep the card current as outcomes, health and updates change
- Portfolio reviews — to compare products on the same terms

## How to access

Available at [pulse-reportcards.vercel.app](https://pulse-reportcards.vercel.app). Sign in and choose **Create with AI** to draft a card from your existing material, such as your PRD, one-pager and test results.

Before you publish, check that everything on the card is of an appropriate classification and check with your programme manager. A published card is public by default; if it should not be, publish it privately instead.

## Output expectations

- Every figure is labelled with its evidence, and only figures from your sources appear
- The card is reviewed across its five tabs — Header, Overview, Outcomes, Health and Updates — before it is published
