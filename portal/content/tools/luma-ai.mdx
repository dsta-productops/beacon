## What it does

Luma generates 3D content and video from plain-language descriptions. Engineering teams use it to make fly-throughs of platforms, immersive visualisations of scenarios, and motion sequences. These help operators engage with a capability concept before any physical prototype exists.

Pairing Luma with an operational scenario from the [operational-scenario generator](/prompts/operational-scenario-generator) gives the team something visual and a story together. Operators can engage with both from start to finish.

## When to use it

- Design phase for engineering teams
- Concept development: to make the space and time aspects of a capability tangible
- Sessions with operators, where a flat image does not convey the experience

## How to access

[lumalabs.ai](https://lumalabs.ai). Cloud-hosted.

## Output expectations

- Treat outputs as conceptual visualisations, not engineering specifications
- Check dimensions and physics with domain experts before any decision depends on them
