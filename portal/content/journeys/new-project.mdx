You are in your first week of leading a new project. There is a brief from management, maybe a few slide decks and some informal conversations. What you do not have yet is a defined product, confirmed users, or a recorded decision about *what* to build.

The temptation will be to jump straight to designing solutions. Resist it for two or three days. The work below is the cheapest part of the whole project, and it has the biggest effect on everything that follows.
