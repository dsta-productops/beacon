A negative test result is information, not a verdict. The first reaction, "the test was wrong", is sometimes true. But jumping straight to it is how teams convince themselves that their framing is fine when it is not.

The work below is diagnostic. Before you change anything, work out which of the three places the result points to. Each one leads to a different next step. Skipping the diagnosis costs more rounds than doing it.
