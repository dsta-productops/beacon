Joining a project that is already moving is harder than starting fresh. The urge to "redo the research properly" is almost always wrong. The earlier work is the team's built-up knowledge of the project. Throwing it away costs the team weeks and signals that you do not trust their judgement.

The work below is about reading what the team has already produced, understanding where they are in their loop, and finding a piece of work that helps instead of disrupts. Hold off on forming strong opinions until you have done all six steps.
