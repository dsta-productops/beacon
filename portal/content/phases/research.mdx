## What this phase is for

Research establishes *who* the work is for, *what* they are trying to achieve, and *what would count as a win*.

Throughout this page, an **artefact** means a document or working model that the team produces, such as a persona or a prototype.

Research is the **outer loop** of the flywheel. The team does it thoroughly at the start of a programme or workstream, and returns to it when measurement findings suggest the problem itself has changed. Once work moves into Design, the inner loop takes over. Design and Measure repeat quickly, and the team goes back to Research only when something fundamental shifts.

For digital teams, this phase typically runs from before the AOR through the planning intervals. For engineering teams, it runs from concept development through to refining the capability requirements.

## How to run a Research prompt

Every Research prompt on this site is a one-line instruction that you paste into an AI assistant with CLARA enabled. CLARA then:

1. Confirms which artefact you want.
2. Asks for the few inputs it cannot work out for itself.
3. Reads the relevant pages.
4. Drafts the artefact.
5. Files it in the programme's Knowledge Base, under the agreed path.
