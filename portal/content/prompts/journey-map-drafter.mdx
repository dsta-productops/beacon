## What CLARA will ask you for

- **Programme name** — your programme's name (for example SKYPROTECT).
- **Track** — the part of the programme this document belongs to (for example a workstream, capability area or feature line), or `Programme-wide` if it spans several. The [CLARA page](/tools/clara) explains tracks.
- **Persona** — a page reference, or the persona's name.
- **Journey scope** — be specific: "submitting an incident report from the field", not "using the system".
- **Inputs** — CLARA will search your programme's Confluence space for the persona for the journey, interview transcripts and observation notes, and optionally themes and friction points. You can also paste in new material that is not in Confluence yet.

## Where the output lands

`Knowledge Base/{{track}}/Journeys/{{journey-scope}}` inside your programme's Confluence space.

## Tips

- The aim is a map of today. A map of the future is a different document, and a different conversation.
- "Moments of truth" are the steps where the persona forms a lasting impression. There are usually 2 to 4 per journey. If you find more than 5, you are mixing them up with friction points.
- Pair the output with a picture from [Napkin AI](/tools/napkin-ai). Text-to-diagram works well for journey maps.
- The output feeds the [service-blueprint drafter](/prompts/service-blueprint-drafter) for digital teams.
