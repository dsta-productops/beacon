## What CLARA will ask you for

- **Programme name** — your programme's name (for example SKYPROTECT).
- **Track** — the part of the programme this document belongs to (for example a workstream, capability area or feature line), or `Programme-wide` if it spans several. The [CLARA page](/tools/clara) explains tracks.
- **Persona name** — a short label for the persona (for example "Field operator" or "Watch officer").
- **Inputs** — CLARA will search your programme's Confluence space for interview transcripts, observation notes or survey responses, and optionally themes and friction points from the research synthesis. You can also paste in new material that is not in Confluence yet.

## Where the output lands

`Knowledge Base/{{track}}/Personas/{{persona-name}}` inside your programme's Confluence space.

## Tips

- Do not ask for several personas at once. Generate one at a time and let each earn its place.
- The "non-obvious trait" check matters because personas blur together when every one is a "busy professional who values efficiency". The trait that sets a persona apart is what makes it useful in design discussions.
- The persona feeds directly into the [journey-map drafter](/prompts/journey-map-drafter), the [service-blueprint drafter](/prompts/service-blueprint-drafter) and the [PRD generator](/prompts/prd-generator).
