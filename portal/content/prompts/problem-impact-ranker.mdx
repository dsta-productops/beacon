## What CLARA will ask you for

- **Programme name** — your programme's name (for example SKYPROTECT).
- **Track** — the part of the programme this document belongs to (for example a workstream, capability area or feature line), or `Programme-wide` if it spans several. The [CLARA page](/tools/clara) explains tracks.
- **Problem set scope** — which group of problems you are ranking (for example "all problems across the end-to-end journey", or one track's friction). One line.
- **Source of the problems** — where the captured problems come from (the friction table in the research synthesis, a workshop board or a pasted list). It is named in the output so the ranking can be traced.
- **Inputs** — CLARA will search your programme's Confluence space for friction points, themes and the problem statement from the research synthesis, personas for the affected roles, optionally prior-knowledge summaries, the raw field notes or other sources of captured problems, and optionally journeys and service blueprints. You can also paste in new material that is not in Confluence yet.

## Where the output lands

`Knowledge Base/{{track}}/Problem-impact-analysis` inside your programme's Confluence space.

## Tips

- The ranking is a **queue of actions, not a list of complaints**. Its value is the sort order and the tiers. An unranked pile of problems does not tell the team where to start.
- **Severity, reach and frequency are each rated High, Medium or Low** (worth 3, 2 or 1 point), and the score is their sum out of 9. A problem rated High on all three ranks first. Ties are broken by severity, then reach, then frequency.
- **Evidence and clusters are shown, not scored.** A persuasive problem backed only by 💬 discussion keeps its rank but is flagged `[provisional]`. That gap tells research what to go and prove next. A cluster of problems with one root cause should usually be tackled as a single product, not as many separate fixes. That judgement is the whole point of the "Clusters" note.
- **A missing rating is shown as `?`, not as Low.** It sorts as Medium and is flagged, so problems nobody has measured are not quietly pushed down.
- This page is the handover between synthesis and build. The research synthesis says *what is wrong*, this document says *which wrong thing to fix first*, and the [PRD generator](/prompts/prd-generator) builds for the top-ranked problem (or the one you choose). Keep the problem statements clean enough that the PRD can lift one straight out.
