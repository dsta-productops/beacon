## What CLARA will ask you for

- **Programme name** — your programme's name (for example SKYPROTECT).
- **Track** — the part of the programme this document belongs to (for example a workstream, capability area or feature line), or `Programme-wide` if it spans several. The [CLARA page](/tools/clara) explains tracks.
- **Journey** — the page under `Knowledge Base/{{track}}/Journeys/*`.
- **Inputs** — CLARA will search your programme's Confluence space for the journey map for the same scope, the persona for the journey, and the system context (the systems, tools and back-stage teams involved). You can also paste in new material that is not in Confluence yet.

## Where the output lands

`Knowledge Base/{{track}}/Service blueprints/{{journey-scope}}` inside your programme's Confluence space.

## Tips

- Service blueprints show the cost of design choices that the journey map hides. A "simpler" front stage often means more back-stage work, not less.
- The handoffs section is where the most surprising findings tend to appear. A back-stage handover that passes through three teams is a place where things break.
- Pair the output with a diagram from [Napkin AI](/tools/napkin-ai), so the layers are visually distinct.
