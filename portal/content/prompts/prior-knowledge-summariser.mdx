## What CLARA will ask you for

- **Programme name** — your programme's name (for example SKYPROTECT).
- **Track** — the part of the programme this document belongs to (for example a workstream, capability area or feature line), or `Programme-wide` if it spans several. The [CLARA page](/tools/clara) explains tracks.
- **Topic** — the topic or area you are researching. Be specific: "scheduling operator interviews around shift patterns", not "user research".
- **Inputs** — CLARA will search your programme's Confluence space for write-ups from past programmes that touch on the topic. You can also paste in new material that is not in Confluence yet.

## Where the output lands

`Knowledge Base/{{track}}/Prior-knowledge/{{topic}}` inside your programme's Confluence space.

## Tips

- Be specific with the topic. Broad questions return vague results.
- If the response is shallow, follow up with: "Go deeper on the recurring patterns — what specifically were the failure modes?"
- Treat the citations as the deliverable. If a finding has no citation, treat it as a hypothesis (an unproven idea to be checked), not as a fact.
- Confirm CLARA's list of sources before it writes the summary. Wrong pages picked up here carry through into every later document, so catch it early.
