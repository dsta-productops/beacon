# INSIGHT practice interview: SKYPROTECT

Use this script to try INSIGHT on your own, without a real participant. You play both parts. Read the **Interviewer** lines in your own voice. Read the **Operator** lines in a different voice, as if you were a console operator at Site Alpha. Say each line out loud, with a short pause between them, so INSIGHT hears a conversation.

The session takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start (optional prep in INSIGHT)

**Upload:** the SKYPROTECT kickoff brief, copied from Confluence into a document.

**Add these terms to the glossary:**

- **Track:** an object that the radar follows over time
- **Emitter:** a radio source that the RF sensor detects. It is the RF tool's word for a contact.
- **EO/IR:** the electro-optical and infrared cameras
- **Soft response:** a response that does not damage the drone, such as jamming
- **Ghost track:** a track that stays on screen after its object has gone
- **ROE:** rules of engagement

## The conversation

**Interviewer:** Thanks for making the time. I'm looking at how operators confirm a drone today. Could you start by walking me through your last busy shift?

**Operator:** Sure. Mornings are the worst. At dawn, the radar lights up with small slow tracks near the shoreline. Mostly gulls, I think.

**Interviewer:** How do you decide which of those to look at?

**Operator:** I don't, really. I've got a rule. If it's small and slow and over the water, I ignore it unless the RF window shows something too.

**Interviewer:** And if the RF window shows nothing?

**Operator:** Then I'm relying on the camera, and I have to point that by hand. By the time I've slewed it, the thing has moved.

**Interviewer:** You said "think" about the gulls. How sure are you?

**Operator:** The radar labels each track bird, drone or unknown. I don't trust the label. It's a guess. I treat unknown as a bird until something else tells me otherwise.

**Interviewer:** Tell me about the last time you did confirm a drone.

**Operator:** A hobby quadcopter, near the east fence. I had a radar track, then RF showed an emitter, and I couldn't tell if they were the same object. Radar calls it a track, RF calls it an emitter. I did the bearing in my head.

**Interviewer:** What happened after you were sure?

**Operator:** I called the commander on the voice line. It rang for a while. On a busy day it can ring for most of a minute, and that's a long time with a confirmed drone.

**Interviewer:** What would you change first?

**Operator:** One list that tells me radar and RF see something at the same bearing. And put a clock on every track, so I know how old it is. Ghost tracks look real.

**Interviewer:** Anything I haven't asked that I should have?

**Operator:** Handover. We use a whiteboard and a chat group. Things get lost. Ask about that next time.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. That's very helpful.

## What to try in INSIGHT

1. Watch the **Now** card while you read the Operator lines. Does it suggest a follow-up question where the operator mentions the label, the commander call or handover?
2. Check that the glossary panel explains **track**, **emitter** and **ghost track** when they come up.
3. End the session and open the review. Check that the speakers are labelled and that the cues have timestamps.
4. Export the review as Markdown.
